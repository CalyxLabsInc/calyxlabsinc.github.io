Privacy Policy for Calyx Labs Inc

​Effective Date: October 20, 2026

​Calyx Labs Inc ("we," "our," or "us") operates mobile applications published on the Google Play Store. This Privacy Policy is designed to inform you about our data privacy practices and to assure you of our commitment to protecting your personal information.

​Information Collection and Use: We respect your privacy. Calyx Labs Inc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data through our mobile applications.

​No Data Collection: We do not track, collect, or log any personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
​Local Storage Only: Any user-generated data, preferences, or profile inputs (such as usernames or settings) are stored strictly and securely in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers, cloud storage, or any third-party services.
​
Third-Party Services: Because our applications do not collect data, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third parties. Our apps do not integrate third-party analytics tools, tracking pixels, or advertising networks that collect data regarding your usage habits.

​Children's Privacy: Our applications do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since our applications operate entirely offline or locally without data transmission, they are safe for users of all age groups.

​Security: Although we do not collect or store your data on external servers, we design our applications following secure development practices to ensure that any locally stored information remains protected on your personal device.

​Changes to This Privacy Policy: We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
