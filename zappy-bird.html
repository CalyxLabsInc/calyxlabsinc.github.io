Power-Up System & Multiplayer

10-Tube Power-Up Arsenal & Modes

Clear 10 tubes to charge your Power-Up meter (`POWER-UP: 10/10 TUBES`) and unleash electric abilities yielding +3 to +10 bonus tube points.

POWER-UP 01 // DESTRUCTIVE SURGE

⚡ E-Bomb

Detonates a high-voltage electromagnetic pulse that vaporizes incoming green pipe obstacles in your path and awards instant bonus clearance points.

POWER-UP 02 // QUANTUM BLINK

🌀 Teleporter

Warps Zappy Bird safely across tight pipe corridors in a flash of cyan lightning, granting temporary phase immunity and +3 to +10 tube progression.

POWER-UP 03 // KINETIC BOOST

🚀 Super Jump

Launches Zappy Bird with precision high-arc elevation control and stabilized glide physics to conquer rapid vertical pipe shifts effortlessly.

GAME MODE // SOLO PLAY

Solo High-Score Chase

Test your reflexes across dynamic Day and Crescent-Moon Night cityscapes with customizable MODE: AUTO-USE power-up deployment and Play Store profile badge display.

GAME MODE // WIFI VERSUS

Local WiFi Versus Battles

Challenge friends head-to-head over local WiFi with zero external servers or lag. Outlast your opponent as tube speeds and power-up combos escalate.

PRIVACY // ZERO TRACKING

100% Zero-Data Gaming

Just like our cryptographic tools, Zappy Bird collects zero personal data, zero location telemetry, and zero third-party ad network profiles.
