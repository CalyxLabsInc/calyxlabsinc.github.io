Privacy Policy
1. Strictly Local On-Device Cryptographic Execution

Calyx QPEP Client executes 100% of all key generation (`QLX` + `HGC-448` + `QVS`), passphrase policy witness derivation (`QPKD-Argon2id + qSponge-512 (kdf_version: 4)`), notepad encryption/decryption (`CC-512`), file streaming encryption, and signature verification locally on your Android device. Calyx Labs Inc. never collects, transmits, or has access to your private keys, passphrases, or plaintext data.

All personal and receiver `.qpkrf` key rings are stored inside your device's private application keystore (`IndexedDB` + persistent DOM storage in `/data/user/0/com.calyxlabs.qpepclient/`) with Android cloud auto-backup disabled (`android:allowBackup="false"`). Your private keys remain encrypted at rest under `QPKD-Argon2id + qSponge-512 (kdf_version: 4)` + `CC-512`.

Network communication occurs only when you explicitly select "Publish Key to Calyx Labs QPEP Keys Directory" (`https://qpepkeys.calyxlabsinc.org`). Only your public `.qpkrf` key ring, Key Hash, name, and email are transmitted so contacts can verify your signatures and encrypt packets to you. Your private key ring and passphrase never leave your device.
